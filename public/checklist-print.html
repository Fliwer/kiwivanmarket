Campervan Inspection Checklist — New Zealand

🥝 kiwivanmarket.com

Follows a real viewing in order, from the night before to the handover. Full explanations: kiwivanmarket.com/guide/campervan-buyer-checklist

1 · The night before (from your hostel)

  • CarJam run (plate) — owners, odometer history, WOF record
  • PPSR run ($3, ppsr.govt.nz, VIN) — no finance owing on the van
  • Price compared to similar listings — under-market = a question, not a win
  • Two messages sent — service record photos? + no engine warm-up before I arrive
  • Viewing booked in daylight, dry weather if possible

2 · First five minutes on site

  • Stance — van sits level, no sagging corner
  • Tyres — matching brands, even wear across all four
  • Smell test inside — no damp odour on first opening
  • Ownership story asked — to match against CarJam dates later

3 · Walk-around: body & rust

  • Panel gaps & paint tone even — no hidden accident repairs
  • Magnet test on sills/arches — sticks everywhere (no filler)
  • Underbody with a torch — rails, floor pans, sills: no structural rust
  • Window rubbers / roof seams — no bubbling paint around glass
  • Under carpets/mats — floor solid, no rot

4 · Bonnet up, then the test drive

  • Bonnet cold before start — genuine cold start, clean idle, no lasting smoke
  • Oil cap & dipstick — no creamy residue; coolant clean, never oily
  • Cambelt history — replacement date/kms known (receipt ideally)
  • Motorway at 100 — no shake through the wheel
  • A real hill — temperature steady, power holds
  • Speed bump slow — no clunks; corners settle after a push
  • Firm straight brake — no pull, no squeal; radio OFF while listening
  • All gears twice incl. reverse — no crunch, no hesitation

5 · Camper kit: test, don't admire

  • Self-contained certificate sighted — valid, matches THIS van
  • Water in / water out — tap runs, drain flows, wood under sink firm
  • Phone charged from every 12V/USB socket
  • Fridge + lights on battery 10 min, engine off — battery age known
  • Solar numbers — panel wattage + controller brand given
  • Gas locker — no smell; compliance certificate if cooker/heater
  • Mattress lifted — no brown rings on the plywood

6 · Money & paperwork (last 15 minutes)

  • WOF + REGO labels current, months from expiry
  • RUC label covers odometer (diesel)
  • Receipt written & signed ×2 — price, date, names, passports, plate, VIN, 'sold as seen'
  • Ownership transferred together, same day (nzta.govt.nz, ~$9)
  • Insurance active before driving away

Walk away if…

  • No independent inspection allowed · timeline pressure · story ≠ CarJam dates · 'mechanic mate checked it' instead of documents · cash-today-only discount · VINs don't match
