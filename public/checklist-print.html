Campervan Inspection Checklist — New Zealand

🥝 kiwivanmarket.com

Viewing prompts, followed by an independent inspection. Updated 15 October 2026. Full explanations: kiwivanmarket.com/guide/campervan-buyer-checklist

1 · The night before (from your hostel)

  • Vehicle identity and records — plate, VIN, service history; check third-party report scope and fees
  • PPSR search (ppsr.govt.nz) — read registered security interests; resolve any result before buying
  • Price compared to similar listings — under-market = a question, not a win
  • Two messages sent — service record photos? + no engine warm-up before I arrive
  • Viewing booked in daylight, dry weather if possible

2 · First five minutes on site

  • Stance — van sits level, no sagging corner
  • Tyres — correct specification, tread, condition and wear patterns for the inspector to assess
  • Smell test inside — no damp odour on first opening
  • Ownership story asked — to match against CarJam dates later

3 · Walk-around: body & rust

  • Panel gaps & paint tone — record differences and ask about repairs; appearance cannot rule out damage
  • Previous body repairs — invoices and photos; a magnet cannot prove sound structure
  • Underbody — inspect from a safe position; never crawl under an unsupported vehicle; arrange professional access
  • Window rubbers / roof seams — no bubbling paint around glass
  • Under carpets/mats — floor solid, no rot

4 · Bonnet up, then the test drive

  • Bonnet cold before start — genuine cold start, clean idle, no lasting smoke
  • Oil and coolant — check safely; never open a hot cooling system; contamination needs a mechanic's diagnosis
  • Exact engine and maintenance — belt or chain, invoices, manufacturer's schedule and work due
  • Safe test route — suitable speed and conditions; note vibration or warning lights
  • A real hill — temperature steady, power holds
  • Speed bump slow — no clunks; corners settle after a push
  • Braking and steering — safe observations only; ask the inspector to investigate pull, noise or vibration
  • All gears twice incl. reverse — no crunch, no hesitation

5 · Camper kit: test, don't admire

  • Self-contained claim — current certificate, green warrant and SCV register; blue warrants are no longer valid
  • Water in / water out — tap runs, drain flows, wood under sink firm
  • Phone charged from every 12V/USB socket
  • Fridge, lights and battery — test operation; record type, capacity and charging; a short test cannot prove battery health
  • Solar numbers — panel wattage + controller brand given
  • Gas safety — documents and qualified gasfitter if uncertain; stop if gas is suspected; no portable gas appliances inside or under awnings
  • Mattress lifted — no brown rings on the plywood

6 · Money & paperwork

  • WoF date and expiry; rego status — buyer can insist on WoF under one month old; follow NZTA rules for older/expired inspections
  • RUC, if applicable — distance covers odometer; resolve unpaid charges; unused RUC remains with the vehicle
  • Written sale and cleared payment — parties, price, date, plate, VIN, equipment, faults and agreed conditions; keep copies
  • Both NZTA notifications immediately; buyer without NZ licence uses an agent; registration does not prove legal ownership
  • Insurance active before driving away

Walk away if…

  • No independent inspection allowed · timeline pressure · story ≠ CarJam dates · 'mechanic mate checked it' instead of documents · cash-today-only discount · VINs don't match
